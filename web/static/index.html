    How the test works

    Every 4h close: your entry, stop and target become a shape scaled by 4h ATR, placed at every 4h close (one order or trade at a time). It tests the distances, not the reason you took the trade.

    Pattern only: the tester finds supply zones (for shorts) or demand zones (for longs) by rule: 1-6 small candles, then a strong move of at least 1 ATR away. When price is away from a fresh zone it rests a limit at the zone's near edge, stop beyond the far edge, target at your R:R. Only candles already closed are used.

    Entry styles (pattern only): sweep waits for price to poke past the zone's far edge (where stops sit) and close back inside; confirm waits for a 15m candle to close through the last 15m swing after the tap. Both put the stop beyond the move's extreme and the zone.

    Both modes are simulated on 15m MFP candles with MFP fees. If TP and SL hit in the same candle it counts as a loss.

    Ready when you are
