      Position sizing optional, for money figures

      Optional. Only takes a setup when the higher timeframe agrees: on its last closed candle, price is above a rising EMA(20) for a long (below a falling one for a short). Only candles already closed at the signal are used.

      Costs and execution

      MFP trading fees and overnight swap are always included. Slippage is the extra price lost on market-type fills: market or stop entries, stop-loss exits and time exits. Limit entries and take profits fill at their own price. 1 bps = 0.01% of price; ticks use the market's tick size.

      How the test works

      Similar moments (default): the tester fingerprints the last N candles at your setup moment (the last time price traded at your entry, or now if it is still there). The path is scale-free, and it also records trend, volatility, swing distance, sweeps and impulse. It then searches the past of the same market for the closest non-overlapping windows, places your entry, stop and single target scaled by 4h ATR, and reports a similarity score and the date of each match so you can check them on a chart.

      Every candle close: your entry, stop and target become a shape scaled by the setup timeframe's ATR, placed at every close of that timeframe (4h by default; one order or trade at a time). It tests the distances, not the reason you took the trade.

      Timeframes: the setup timeframe (5m to daily) sets where setups are looked for. Fills are always simulated on the finest reliable candles: 5m for 5m setups, 15m for everything else. Higher-timeframe confirmation reads only higher-timeframe candles already closed at the signal.

      Pattern only: a rule-based approximation of supply or demand zones (1-6 small candles, then a strong move of at least 1 ATR away). When price is away from a fresh zone it rests a limit at the near edge, stop beyond the far edge, target at your R:R. This is optional and is not how most order-flow, ICT or SMC setups are tested. Only candles already closed are used.

      Entry styles (pattern only): sweep waits for price to poke past the far edge (where stops sit) and close back inside; confirm waits for a 15m candle to close through the last 15m swing after the tap.

      All modes are simulated on 15m MFP candles with MFP fees and your slippage setting. Orders are decided at a candle close using only candles that had already closed; fills and exits are read from later candles only. If TP and SL are both inside one candle, the order inside that candle is unknown, so it counts as a loss. A take profit touched in the same candle the order filled is not counted. A gap through the stop exits at the gap price, not at the stop. One order or trade at a time unless you allow overlapping trades.

      Theoretical vs after costs: theoretical results use the exact fill and exit prices with no fees or slippage. After-costs results take off MFP fees, swap and slippage. The verdict uses after-costs results.
